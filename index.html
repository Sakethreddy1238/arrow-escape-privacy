Arrow Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026  ·  App ID com.sakethreddy.ArrowEscape

Arrow Escape is a single-player puzzle game. It was built to be played offline, and it does not ask you to create an account or give us any personal information. This policy explains, plainly, what the app handles and who it works with.

The short version

  • We never ask for your name, email, or an account.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases (such as Premium) are handled by Google Play — we never see your payment details.
  • We run no servers and no analytics of our own.

Information the app handles

On-device game data

Your level progress, stars, coins, settings, and whether you own Premium are saved locally on your device. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and your interactions with ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under your device's Settings → Google → Ads. Buying Premium removes all ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment; we never receive or store your card or payment details. The app only learns whether the Premium entitlement is owned, so it can unlock Premium features and restore them if you reinstall.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (around 9 AM and 9 PM). These are created on your device — no notification data is sent to us or to any server.

Feedback you choose to send

If you rate the app and choose to send feedback, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message to the developer. Sending it is entirely your choice; only if you send it do we receive your message and email address, so we can read and reply.

How information is used

Information is used only to run and save your game, show ads in the free version, process and restore purchases, deliver reminders you turned on, and respond to feedback you send. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Who information is shared with

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with the services that make the above features work, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob / Google — to serve ads in the free version.
  • Google Play — to process and verify purchases.

Data retention

On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We keep no copy of it, because the app has no servers. Information handled by Google for ads and purchases is retained according to Google's policies.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Remove all on-device data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.
  • Buy Premium to remove ads entirely.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. As we hold no personal data on our own servers, such requests mainly concern Google; you can also contact us and we'll help where we can.

Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Ads and purchases are handled over Google's secured services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective” date above shows when it last changed, and material changes will be reflected on this page.
